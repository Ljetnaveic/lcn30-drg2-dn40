1LCN30 · DRG 2

DN40 condensate line from DRG 1 through valves 1LCN30AA400 and 1LCN30AA030, down 645 mm into a horizontal expansion zig-zag at EL +4450, to the tie-in on 0LCN50BR200. Three bent-pipe spools, R 200 bends, placed in plant coordinates.

Rebuilt from the site copy of the isometric (with hand mark-up) for visualisation, fit-up and checking. All dimensions are theoretical PI-to-PI values from the iso; verify on site before cutting, as the iso's general remarks require.

Read before use

Assumptions and how to load it

What was read, what was assumed

  1. Orientation comes from the axis legend in the title block (+X drawn up-right, +Y up-left). Confirm against the north arrow on the issued iso; if it differs, the model rotates about FW-1.
  2. Start point is the iso's tie-in: X 91628, Y 82209, EL +4450. Everything is placed from there.
  3. Leg G rises 645, so the valve run sits at EL +5095. That run lies directly above segment C.
  4. FW-2 is taken as 1800 from B2, from the site mark "1800+". FW4 is scaled from the mark-up at about 350 from B5. Measure both on site.
  5. FW-3 is not marked on this sheet. Valve welds VW-1 to VW-3 are numbered here only for reference.
  6. Valve and support shapes are symbolic. Real sizes come from the valve list and the TC021-AFR-…-SU support drawings. Pipe OD is 48.3 (DN40); wall thickness follows class ECJB_TBY.
  7. Beyond valve AA400 the line continues on DRG 1. The grey riser at the DRG 1 PI is assumed from the 3D print and is context only.
  8. Weld status is taken from the hand mark-up: FW-2 welded 03.11.2026 and FW4 welded 05.11.2026, both by welder HC130. FW-1 and FW5 have no record on the sheet.

Load it into Navisworks or AutoCAD

  1. Navisworks Manage / Simulate: open the plant model, then Home › Append and pick 1LCN30_DRG2_DN40_3D_Model.dxf (units mm) or the .fbx. It lands at its real X / Y / EL, inside the existing model.
  2. Navisworks Freedom only opens NWD files and cannot append. Someone with Manage appends the file and saves an NWD; Freedom then opens it.
  3. AutoCAD / Plant 3D / BricsCAD: open the 3D DXF in model space (WCS = plant coordinates). Layers split spools, insulation, valves, supports and welds. The 2D DXF holds all seven sheets side by side.
  4. Blender / other tools: use the .obj or .fbx (Z up, mm).
